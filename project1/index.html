Santa’s Chocolate Chip Cookies

These are a more decadent, traditional chocolate chip cookie. Anytime I’ve had a rough day I make these and they are so warm, gooey, and comforting!

Ingredients:

1 1/4 cup all purpose (or gf 1:1 flour)

1/4 cup classic Lakanto sugar

1/2 cup packed Lakanto brown sugar

1 egg

1 tsp baking soda

1 tsp vanilla extract

1/2 tsp salt

1/2 cup melted butter (grass-fed if possible)

3 oz dark chocolate chips

Instructions:

1. Beat butter and both sugars with an electric mixer.

2. Mix in egg and vanilla.

3. In a separate bowl mix flour, salt, and baking soda.

4. Slowly beat dry ingredients into the wet ingredients.

5. Fold in chocolate chips.

6. Refrigerate dough for 1 hour.

7. Bake at 325 for 15 minutes, then immediately take out and let cool 5 minutes (make sure you don’t over-bake or they will get very dry!).

8. Best enjoyed warm!

Chocolate Peppermint Cake with Pitaya Peppermint Frosting

This was my birthday cake for YEARS straight. To this day it is one of my favorites and it always tastes better when mom makes it!

Chocolate Peppermint Cake

2 cups gluten-free 1:1 baking flour

1 teaspoon baking soda

2 teaspoons baking powder

1/2 teaspoon salt

1 cup date sugar

1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder

1/4 cup agave syrup (or maple syrup)

1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract

1/2 teaspoon pure peppermint extract

1 can aquafaba (the liquid in a can of chickpeas)

2 teaspoons cream of tartar (whipped with the chickpea brine)

1 can coconut milk

1/2 cup dark chocolate chunks/chips

Pitaya Peppermint Frosting

1 cup vegan/plant butter

4 cups organic powdered sugar

1 teaspoon pure peppermint extract

2 tablespoons Suncore Foods Pink Pitaya Supercolor Powder

Decorations (Sugared Cranberries/Rosemary Sprigs)

1 cup fresh organic cranberries

Fresh rosemary (about 4-6 sprigs)

1/2 cup organic cane sugar

1/2 cup water

1-2 cups sparkling sugar

Sugared Cranberries + Rosemary

If you're decorating your cake with sugared cranberries and rosemary, make these first.

Place 1/2 cup granulated sugar and 1/2 cup water in a small saucepan. Stir together while heating over medium-high heat.

Continue to stir occasionally until the sugar dissolves and the mixture begins to boil.

Remove from heat and add the cranberries (you may have to do this in a couple of different batches).

Allow the cranberries to sit for 4-5 minutes in the sugar water mixture.

Using a slotted spoon, remove cranberries from the saucepan and place on parchment paper for 10 minutes (making sure they aren't touching).

Repeat with other cranberries and the rosemary sprigs.

Place your sparkling sugar in a medium bowl.

Add a few cranberries at a time and use your fingers or a small spoon to gently roll them in the sugar to coat them completely on all sides (be sure your cranberries don't stick to each other).

Remove and place on a parchment-lined baking rack to dry.

Repeat with all cranberries and rosemary sprigs.

Let them dry at room temperature for one hour. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Chocolate Peppermint Cake

Preheat oven to 350ºF.

Whisk together all dry ingredients in a medium bowl (except for dark chocolate chunks/chips).

In a mixer or a hand beater with a large bowl, beat your aquafaba (the liquid from a can of chickpeas) on high until it starts to froth up. Add the cream of tartar as it starts to thicken. It's done when stiff peaks form and it resembles whipped egg whites.

Add the liquid ingredients to the bowl with the whipped aquafaba (this will cause it to deflate).

Finally, add in the dry cake mix to the bowl and blend until a nice smooth batter forms.

Fold in the chocolate chunks/chips.

Spray 2 6-inch cake pans with coconut oil and place them on a large baking sheet.

Fill each pan equally with the cake batter and bake for 25-35 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the middle comes out clean.

Move the cakes to a cooling rack and cool for about an hour before frosting.

Pitaya Peppermint Frosting

In a mixer or bowl with a hand blender, beat the butter, vanilla, and peppermint until it's smooth.

Slowly add the powdered sugar, 1/2 cup at a time, and continue beating as you go.

Finally, add the Pitaya Powder.

Frosting and Decorating

When your cakes are cool, place one cake onto a cake platter or flat dish.

Add a generous amount of frosting on the top of that cake.

Add the other cake on top of it, making sure it's stable.

Spread the remaining frosting over the whole cake.

Decorate with sugared cranberries and rosemary sprigs.

Jam Dot Cookies

My absolute go to cookie! These are a Christmas Eve staple that everyone asks for extras to take home.

Ingredients:

2 ½ cups flour

1 ½ tsp baking soda

¼ tsp salt

1 cup butter, softened

1 ½ cups sugar

1 egg

1 TBS vanilla extract

½ cup strawberry jam

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F. and line a baking tray with foil.

2. In a medium bowl, stir together flour, baking soda, salt.

3. In large bowl, cream together butter, sugar and vanilla with electric mixer. Then add eggs.

4. Add flour mix to butter mix and beat until combined.

5. Using your hands, roll the dough into 1-inch balls and place 2 inches apart on baking tray. Using your finger, make a light indent on the center of each cookie and fill with a dollop of jam.

6. Bake 15-20 minutes or until cookies are done. Remove cookies from oven and let cool 10 minutes. Enjoy!
